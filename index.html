About Vertex Math Program

Vertex Math Program is a no-cost contest-preparation program based at the Coquitlam City Centre Library, designed for motivated students in Grades 9 – 12 who wish to develop their problem-solving abilities and prepare for the CEMC mathematics contests.

Each month focuses on one contest — Pascal, Cayley, and Fermat — through authentic past problems, proven strategies, and the problem-solving techniques that the standard curriculum does not have time to cover. Students may attend to strengthen their skills, address specific gaps, or take on a greater challenge.

Each session is structured to prepare students for the February contest season. No prior contest experience is required.
GRADE 9 – 12  |  CONTEST PREP

Small-Group Instruction

Focused sessions that allow for individual guidance and collaborative problem-solving.

Authentic Contest Preparation

Practice with genuine past problems from the Pascal, Cayley, and Fermat contests.

Strategy & Technique

Develop the timing, estimation, and elimination methods essential to performing under time constraints.

Open to All Levels

Welcoming to all students at no cost, with no prior contest experience required.
